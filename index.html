Privacy Policy

Flow Automator for Google Flow • Last Updated: October 6, 2026

Summary: Flow Automator does not collect, track, transmit, or sell any of your personal data, browser activity, prompts, or generated media. Everything runs 100% locally on your computer inside your browser.

1. Overview

Flow Automator for Google Flow is an independent browser extension developed by Subhan Javed to assist creators with bulk queue automation and downloading on the Google Flow platform. We are deeply committed to respecting and protecting user privacy.

2. Information Collection and Use

Flow Automator adheres strictly to the principle of zero telemetry:

  • No Account Required: You do not need to create an account or provide any personal details to use Flow Automator.
  • No Data Harvesting: We do not collect names, email addresses, passwords, IP addresses, browsing histories, or hardware fingerprints.
  • No Prompt Logging: The prompts, queue scripts, and reference images you input are processed directly inside your browser and are never sent to any external server owned or operated by us.
  • Local Storage Only: Extension preferences (such as aspect ratio selections, download subfolder names, and dark/light mode toggles) are saved strictly on your local device via Chrome's native chrome.storage.local API.

3. Browser Permissions and Justifications

Flow Automator requests only the specific browser permissions necessary to perform its advertised functions:

  • sidePanel: Enables the sidebar user interface alongside Google Flow.
  • storage: Stores your local queue settings and preferences on your device.
  • downloads: Saves generated media files to your Downloads directory.
  • tabs & scripting: Detects your active Google Flow workspace tab and communicates between the side panel and the editor.
  • notifications: Alerts you on your desktop when a generation queue finishes.

4. Third-Party Services and External Websites

The extension includes optional external links for user convenience:

  • Buy Me a Coffee: If you choose to support the developer, payments and donations are processed directly by Buy Me a Coffee (buymeacoffee.com/subhanjaved) according to their privacy policy. No financial or card data is ever visible to or collected by Flow Automator.
  • LinkedIn: Links to the developer's public profile for support and professional contact.
  • Google Flow: Flow Automator operates on Google Flow web pages. Your interactions with Google Flow are governed by Google's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

5. Children's Privacy

Flow Automator does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13 years of age.
